# omaiverse series playbooks

Six series, one brand. Every reel follows `frame.md` and the shared edit grammar (`../../references/edit-grammar.md`: layouts L1-L8, recipes R1-R8, transitions T1-T8, plus T9-flash in Clubs). Each playbook below fixes what changes per series: hook patterns, beat structure, visuals, voice, music and CTA. Hook text is 6 words max on screen.

Shared defaults: 20-45 s, English, the series chip from frame 1, one volt accent per frame, series CTA on the end card, the reel loops.

## 1. AI Breakdown

Chip `AI BREAKDOWN` · signal. What a new AI model, tool or headline actually means, and what to do with it.

- **Hooks:** "This AI just replaced [job/tool]" · "[Tool] dropped. Here's what matters." · "Everyone is wrong about [AI news]" · "3 AI tools nobody is using yet".
- **Beats:** R1 hook (L2, a screenshot of the news or tool in the stage) → R2 stat punch (the one number: price, speed, benchmark) → 3× R3 tool demo or R4 list beat ("what it does / who it's for / the catch") → R7 reveal (the result on screen) → R8.
- **Visuals:** real screen recordings in screen cards, product UI, a chart redrawn in kit colours. T8 glitch allowed once for the "AI just did it" moment.
- **Voice:** narrated, `af_heart` (or the chosen narrator), speed 1.05-1.1. Calm, sure, no hype words.
- **Music:** minimal electronic or lo-fi, 100-120 BPM, at 0.14 under the voice.
- **Rule:** date-stamp news in a source chip ("Announced Oct 2026"). Say what is confirmed and what is rumour.

## 2. Claude Skills

Chip `CLAUDE SKILLS` · clay. One Claude feature, skill or workflow per reel, shown working.

- **Hooks:** "Stop using Claude like this" · "This Claude skill saves me hours" · "Claude can do this. Nobody knows." · "I built [thing] with one Claude prompt".
- **Beats:** R1 hook (L3, the finished result in a screen card) → R5 before/after (manual way vs Claude way) → R6 prompt → result (L8 prompt card typed on, T6 blur swap to the output) → 1-2× R3 tool demo of the steps → R7 reveal → R8.
- **Visuals:** real Claude UI or terminal screen recordings, prompts in JetBrains Mono on prompt cards so viewers can pause and copy. Pin the full prompt in the caption or comments.
- **Voice:** narrated, speed 1.0-1.05; slow down on the prompt itself.
- **Music:** focus/lo-fi, 90-110 BPM.
- **Rule:** show Anthropic's real interface as the subject, never Anthropic's logo or colours as omaiverse branding. "Claude" is spoken as "Clawd" by the lexicon.

## 3. SDE & AI Jobs

Chip `SDE & AI JOBS` · cyan. Roles, skills, pay, interviews and how to get hired in software and AI.

- **Hooks:** "This AI role pays [X] LPA" · "Skills every SDE needs in 2027" · "I'd learn this before DSA" · "Your resume is losing you interviews".
- **Beats:** R1 hook → R2 stat punch (salary or demand number, with source chip) → R4 list beat (3 skills or 3 steps, mono chips for tech names) → R5 before/after (resume line, interview answer) → R8 with "save this" CTA.
- **Visuals:** job-post screenshots with personal details blurred, a roadmap drawn as an L5 list, code snippets in mono.
- **Voice:** narrated, speed 1.05, coach-like.
- **Music:** upbeat minimal, 110-125 BPM.
- **Rule:** every salary carries a source chip with month and year and a range, not a single promise. No company's hiring claims without a public source.

## 4. MBA & Business Breakdown

Chip `BUSINESS BREAKDOWN` · gold. How a company makes money, a strategy decision, an MBA concept explained with a real case.

- **Hooks:** "How [company] really makes money" · "Why [company] is losing [X] crore" · "The MBA concept that explains [trend]" · "[Company] did this. It worked."
- **Beats:** R1 hook (L1 brand or product footage, or L4 big number) → R2 stat punch (revenue, loss or margin, source chip) → 3× R4 list beat or L6 split (the framework: model, moat, mistake) → R7 reveal (the insight in one line) → R8.
- **Visuals:** numbers as L4, simple bar or line charts in kit colours (gold only for the series chip; data bars in paper and volt), company product shots, never their logos as our design.
- **Voice:** narrated, speed 1.0-1.05, analyst tone. Numbers spoken in full ("five hundred crore").
- **Music:** cinematic minimal, 90-110 BPM.
- **Rule:** cite the filing, report or article for every number, and say "estimated" where it is.

## 5. Lifestyle

Chip `LIFESTYLE` · mint. Om's day, routines, workspace, travel, the life of an AI creator.

- **Hooks:** "A day as an AI creator in [city]" · "My [time] routine that changed everything" · "What I actually use every day" · text over the strongest shot.
- **Beats:** R1 hook (L1 full-bleed footage, hook in head zone) → 4-8 footage shots of 1-3 s cut on the beat → one L4 or L5 moment for a takeaway (a number or a 3-item list) → R8.
- **Visuals:** real footage with `footage-grade` (warm, natural), idle push on every shot, T1 punch and T2 whip only; no glitch.
- **Voice:** Om's own voice or music-only with on-screen text. If AI narration is used, the same narrator as the knowledge series.
- **Music:** trending audio added in Instagram's editor; export with ambient and voice only.
- **Rule:** captions only where words are spoken; on-screen text otherwise 3-6 words per line in the head zone.

## 6. Clubs

Chip `CLUBS` · magenta. Club nights, events, music, the scene: energy first, words last.

- **Hooks:** the best shot in frame 1 with a 2-4 word title ("Friday in [city]", "Best night of 2026"), or a beat drop at 0.0 s.
- **Beats:** R1 hook on the first beat → 8-15 shots of 0.3-1.5 s cut on the beat, T9 flash on drops (max 3), T1 punch on accents → one L4 moment (venue, date, line-up) → R8.
- **Visuals:** real footage with the Clubs `footage-grade` (shadows toward magenta and signal), motion blur kept, no screen cards.
- **Voice:** none, or one short line from Om. Captions off unless someone speaks.
- **Music:** the club track added in Instagram's editor so it stays licensed; for the export, use the ambient audio only.
- **Rule:** no identifiable stranger as the focus of a shot without their OK; no minors; no glorifying drink or drug use. Tag the venue only if they agreed.
